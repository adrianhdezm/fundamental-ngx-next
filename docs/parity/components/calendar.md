### Gaps to fill

- **Stories:** "Mobile" (buttons that open the calendar in a dialog in landscape and portrait mode, with the close button and an OK footer) and "Open: mobile landscape"; "Legend in a column" (`legendCol`) next to the row legend; add a custom `aggregatedYearGrid` to the year-grid story; "Programmatic date change" (a "Next day" button bound to the model); extend "Forms" to reactive single/multi/range/multi-range controls with touched/dirty/valid and "Set invalid" buttons; "Locale switcher" (segmented button that changes the adapter locale live).
- **Tests:** "the years-range view lists year ranges and Enter selects one"; "grids expose aria-readonly and aria-multiselectable per selection mode"; "range hover marks the days up to the hovered day"; "months with six weeks render six rows"; "today is the tab stop when nothing is selected"; "shift-click without an anchor selects only the clicked date"; "shift-click fills across months and in reverse"; "writing a value resets the shift anchor"; "arrow keys and Enter/Space pick a month and a year"; "ArrowUp on the first row and ArrowLeft on the first cell go to the previous month"; "Space selects the focused day"; "writeValue validates multiple dates, ranges and multiple ranges"; "legend items are list items; the legend is labelled and can be a column; appointment entries show a circle".
- **Effort:** L, about 13 tests across views and 6 stories, one of them a dialog.
