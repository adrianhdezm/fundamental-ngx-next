### Gaps to fill

- **Stories:** "Compact tokens" — standalone compact `fd-token`s (default, selected, read-only) in a compact density container; "Open: overflow" — narrow tokenizer whose `play` clicks the "n more" link and shows the popover list with remove buttons.
- **Tests:** "clicking the close icon emits onCloseClick and does not select the token"; "disabled tokens do not emit onCloseClick and are aria-disabled"; "read-only tokens have no close icon and are not removed by Backspace"; "emits elementFocused on focus and blur"; "sets aria-roledescription"; "Ctrl click toggles a token without clearing the others"; "tokens expand back when the tokenizer gets wider"; "externalHiddenCount adds to the n more label and shows it without hidden tokens"; "n more opens a popover listing hidden tokens that can be removed"; "arrow keys are mirrored in RTL".
